A night at Majlis

How a henna night unfolds.

Every family does it their own way. Here is a typical evening, which your coordinator shapes around your traditions, your timeline and your aunties' opinions.

  1. 6:00 pm

    Guests arrive

    Families are welcomed at the door with green tea, cardamom and bowls of nuql. Elders are seated first, the way it should be.

    KahwaNuqlWelcome sweets
  2. 7:00 pm

    Dinner is served

    Kabuli palaw, kababs fresh off the grill, mantu and bolani. Family-style platters or a full buffet, sized so nobody runs out.

    Kabuli palawMantuLamb tikka
  3. 8:30 pm

    The henna procession

    The lights dim. Family carries the candlelit khancha to the couple's stage while the room sings along. This is the moment everyone films.

    KhanchaCouple's stageCandlelight
  4. 9:00 pm

    The dance floor opens

    Attan circles, dhol and your DJ's playlist. We clear the floor, adjust the lighting and keep the chai coming.

    AttanDholDJ
  5. 10:30 pm

    Sweets & farewells

    Firni, sheer yakh and the dessert table, then qaimaq chai for the long goodbyes at the door. We handle cleanup. You go home.

    FirniSheer yakhQaimaq chai

From our kitchen

Food the elders will approve of.

A halal Afghan menu cooked in-house, from wedding palaw to fresh-folded mantu. Build your menu with our chef, or start from a package.

    SIGN

  • Mantu

    SIG

  • Narenj Palaw

    N

    The wedding rice: saffron, candied orange peel, pistachios and almonds over chicken.

  • Qorma-e Sabzi

    V

How many guests can Majlis hold?
Up to 150 guests seated. Lounge-style henna nights and ceremonies with a stage seat a little fewer. Your coordinator maps the exact layout with you.
Do we have to use your catering?
No. Our in-house Afghan kitchen caters most events, and outside caterers are welcome. Plenty of families mix both: our palaw and kababs with an auntie's famous dessert.
Can we bring our own decorator, DJ and photographer?
Yes. We work alongside Bay Area decorators, DJs, dessert artists and photographers, and we coordinate load-in and timing with each of them.
Is the venue accessible?
Yes. The hall is ADA accessible with Wi-Fi throughout. Tell us about any guest needs and we'll plan seating around them.
How far ahead should we book?
Weekend dates in spring and summer go first, so 3 to 6 months ahead is ideal. Weeknights and short-notice dates often open up. Ask us.
How do we reserve a date?
Send a request with your date and guest count. We'll confirm availability, send a custom quote and walk you through holding the date.
Do you host iftars, Eid and Nawroz events?
Yes. Ramadan iftars, Eid parties and Nawroz celebrations are some of our favorite nights of the year. Book early for those dates.
