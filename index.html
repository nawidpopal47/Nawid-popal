Welcome to Majlis

A gathering place for the families of the Bay Area

In Dari and Pashto, a majlis is the room where everyone sits together. Ours is a freshly renovated hall in Union City with its own Afghan kitchen, built for the celebrations that bring the whole family under one roof.

From a candlelit henna night to an intimate nikkah or a 150-guest wedding reception, one team handles the hall, the food and the details. You get to sit down and enjoy the night.

Catering

From our kitchen to your table

A halal Afghan menu cooked in-house, from wedding palaw to fresh-folded mantu. Choose family-style, buffet or plated service.

  • Kabuli Palaw

    Rice with tender lamb, caramelized carrots, raisins and almonds.

  • Mantu

    Steamed beef dumplings, garlic yogurt, split-pea sauce and dried mint.

  • Narenj Palaw

    The wedding rice: saffron, candied orange peel and pistachios.

  • Qorma-e Sabzi

    Spinach and leeks slow-cooked with lamb or chickpeas.

  • Lamb Tikka Kabab

    Marinated lamb skewers, char-grilled, with naan and chutney.

  • Chapli Kabab

    Flat, spiced beef patties with tomato and coriander.

  • Chicken Tikka Kabab

    Yogurt and garlic marinated chicken over open flame.

  • Chopan Kabab

    Shepherd-style lamb chops, simply seasoned.

  • Bolani

    Flatbread stuffed with potato and green onion, with chutney.

  • Burani Banjan

    Fried eggplant with stewed tomato and garlic yogurt.

  • Sambosa

    Crisp pastries with spiced beef or chickpeas.

  • Shor Nakhod

    Chickpea and potato salad with cilantro vinegar.

  • Firni

    Cardamom and rosewater milk pudding with pistachio.

  • Sheer Yakh

    Afghan ice cream with rosewater and cardamom.

  • Gosh-e Feel

    Crisp "elephant ear" pastry dusted with sugar.

  • Qaimaq Chai

    Rosy, cardamom tea crowned with thick cream.

FAQ

Questions families ask us

Anything else, just call. A real person picks up.

(510) 574-6273
How many guests can Majlis hold?
Up to 150 guests seated. Lounge-style henna nights and ceremonies with a stage seat a little fewer. Your coordinator maps the exact layout with you.
Do we have to use your catering?
No. Our in-house Afghan kitchen caters most events, and outside caterers are welcome. Plenty of families mix both: our palaw and kababs with an auntie's famous dessert.
Can we bring our own decorator, DJ and photographer?
Yes. We work alongside Bay Area decorators, DJs, dessert artists and photographers, and coordinate load-in and timing with each of them.
Is the venue accessible?
Yes. The hall is ADA accessible with Wi-Fi throughout. Tell us about any guest needs and we'll plan seating around them.
How far ahead should we book?
Weekend dates in spring and summer go first, so 3 to 6 months ahead is ideal. Weeknights and short-notice dates often open up. Ask us.
How do we reserve a date?
Send a request with your date and guest count. We'll confirm availability, send a custom quote and walk you through holding the date.
Do you host iftars, Eid and Nawroz events?
Yes. Ramadan iftars, Eid parties and Nawroz celebrations are some of our favorite nights of the year. Book early for those dates.
